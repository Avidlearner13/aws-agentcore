# Guideline G-30: Claims Fraud — Common Red Flags

Weight these signals when scoring fraud risk (0–100):
- Loss reported long after the date of loss, or just after a coverage increase/renewal.
- Amounts that conveniently sit just under a deductible or sub-limit threshold.
- No supporting documentation, or estimates from an unverifiable contractor.
- Prior similar claims by the same claimant (repeat pattern).
- Inconsistent narrative (cause of loss doesn't match the damage pattern).
- Round-number or inflated line items relative to market rates.

Low risk indicators: prompt reporting (within 24–48h), independent contractor estimate, damage
consistent with stated cause, no claim history.
