# Guideline R-05: Regulatory Claims-Handling Rules (Illinois, summary)

- **Prompt acknowledgement:** acknowledge a claim within 15 working days of notice.
- **Timely decision:** accept or deny within a reasonable period after receiving proof of loss;
  if more time is needed, notify the claimant in writing with the reason.
- **Good-faith handling:** unfair claims practices (misrepresenting policy terms, unreasonable delay)
  are prohibited.
- **Fraud referral:** suspected fraudulent claims must be documented and may require referral to the
  state fraud bureau; do not deny solely on suspicion without investigation.
- **PII handling:** claimant personal data must be minimized, masked in logs, and access-controlled.
