# Guideline G-12: Water Damage — Coverage Determination

- **Sudden and accidental** discharge from a plumbing, heating, or AC system (e.g., a burst supply
  pipe) is a **covered peril** under HO-3 dwelling coverage.
- **Flood / surface water** and **water below the surface of the ground** are **excluded** (require
  separate flood coverage).
- **Sewer or drain backup** is excluded unless the **Water Backup & Sump Overflow** endorsement is on
  the policy; that endorsement does NOT apply to internal supply-line bursts.
- **Continuous or repeated seepage** over weeks/months (maintenance) is excluded; a one-time sudden
  burst is not seepage.
- Apply the **All Other Perils deductible** unless a wind/hurricane deductible applies.
