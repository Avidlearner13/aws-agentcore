Enterprise claims-adjudication platform
A customer files an insurance claim. The platform decides three things:
Is it covered?(claim checked against the policy terms)
How risky is it?(fraud score from the claim vs. KB guidelines)
What should we pay?(eligible amount after deductible & limits)
The work is split across three AI agents on three different frameworks (GCP ADK · Claude Agent SDK · LangChain), all running on AWS Bedrock AgentCore.
