Perils Insured Against
Section I covers the Dwelling and Other Structures against risk of direct physical loss (open perils), except as
excluded. Personal Property is covered against the named perils listed in the policy (fire, lightning, windstorm, theft,
etc.).

Exclusions
 Flood and surface water
 Earthquake and earth movement
 Neglect, wear and tear, and gradual deterioration
 Ordinance or law (except as provided by endorsement)
 Intentional loss
Conditions
Insured duties after loss include prompt notice, protecting the property from further damage, preparing an inventory,
and cooperating with the investigation. Loss is payable 60 days after a sworn proof of loss is filed.
